## Design Context

### Users

OpenKara targets casual singers. They use a desktop app to turn their own music files into karaoke tracks. Most users are not technical. They must not need to understand audio-processing terms, storage details, or file-system jargon. They can set up the app, import songs, and sing without this knowledge.

Primary jobs to be done:

- Set up a karaoke library without confusion
- Import songs they already own
- Start playback and sing along quickly
- Recover from errors. Users must not feel blocked or blamed.

The interface must assume users feel uncertain during setup, impatient while they wait, and stressed when something fails.

### Brand Personality

The product voice must feel clean, minimal, and clear. Copy must be calm and low-friction, not playful or technical. The emotional goal is steady confidence. Users must feel guided, reassured, and in control.

### Aesthetic Direction

The app must feel like a focused desktop music tool: native, quiet, and intentional. Prioritize clarity over decoration. Make audio-specific features feel approachable for non-experts. Avoid overly technical language, corporate phrasing, and hype-heavy copy.

### Design Principles

1. Use everyday language before technical terminology.
2. State what happened and what the user can do next.
3. Keep copy brief. Keep copy clear.
4. Match the user's emotional state with calm, direct wording.
5. Use the same term consistently throughout the product.
